Story of the series

Melissa, the mistress of billionaire Jason, in order to obtain Jason's wife's status and his fortune, sends kidnappers to abduct Jason's wife Lisa and daughter Ellie. Unable to contact Jason, the kidnappers decide to livestream the kidnapping to demand a ransom, placing Ellie's daughter in a huge fish tank with continuously flowing water.
