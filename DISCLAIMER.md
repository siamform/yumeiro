# Disclaimer

Yumeiro is a media player. **It does not host, store, upload or distribute any video, audio, TV
channel or other content.**

- Playlists in the Playlist Market and the default stream list are links to **third-party** public
  playlists. They are mirrored automatically from the sources listed in
  [`.github/market/sources.json`](.github/market/sources.json) and
  [`.github/streams/sources.json`](.github/streams/sources.json). The project does not review,
  control or endorse what they point to.
- Extensions (CloudStream `.cs3` plugins) and extension repositories are written and hosted by
  **third parties**. They run code on your device; install only ones you trust.
- You are responsible for making sure that what you play is legal where you live.

Rights holders: to have a playlist source removed from the market, open an issue or email the
maintainer listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/siamform). Removal from the
sources list takes effect on the next market sync (at most a few hours).
