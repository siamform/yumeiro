# Yumeiro name and logo

The source code of Yumeiro is free software under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE). The licence covers the
code — it does **not** grant rights to the project's identity.

The name **"Yumeiro"**, the moon-and-play logo and the app icon are the identity of the official
project by [@dev-asfakul](https://github.com/dev-asfakul). Under section 7(e) of the GPL v3 the
licence does not grant permission to use them, so:

- **Forks are welcome**, but a published fork must use a different name, a different icon and a
  different `applicationId`, and must not present itself as the official Yumeiro.
- **Do not re-upload the official APK** (or a re-signed copy of it) to app stores, Telegram
  channels or mirror sites. Re-signed copies are detected by the app and labelled "Unofficial build".
- You may use the name and logo to *refer* to the project (reviews, articles, videos, links).

The only official downloads are the [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/dev-asfakul/yumeiro/releases)
of this repository, signed with the certificate whose SHA-256 is published in
[`.github/release-signing-cert.sha256`](.github/release-signing-cert.sha256).
